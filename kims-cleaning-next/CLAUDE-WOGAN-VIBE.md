# Claude prompt — Wogan-style eco punch, Kim’s brand

Live: https://kimscleaningproducts.t3kdesigns.app/

Reference craft (colors and logo stay Kim’s): the Wogan Coffee homepage energy — mint field, huge green type, one object on the right, lots of air, one dark button.

Do not become a coffee site. Do not replace the photographed globe with a flat illustration. Do not change PayPal, Venmo, products, prices, or events.

## Keep locked

- Wordmark: Kim’s Cleaning Products + Eco Easy Microfiber
- Header globe crop (the real photo, not clipart)
- Hero photo: the earth + fern
- Palette only:
  - earth `#1E4A22` / `#2F6B32`
  - leaf `#5C9A3A`
  - wash `#E7F4C8` (this is the Wogan mint — use it as a field)
  - cream `#F7F3E8`
  - paper `#FFFFFF`
  - lime CTA `#8BC34A` or current lime button
- Slogan: Cleans with just water. / Wet it. Wring it. Wipe it. Walk away.

## What to steal from Wogan

1. **Page field.** Homepage below the photo hero, and inner pages, should feel like a mint/wash canvas — not a sad cream slab. Alternate wash and paper in big bands.
2. **Type scale.** Headlines bigger, heavier, more tracking-tight. Subcopy smaller and calmer. One idea per heading.
3. **Hero composition (desktop).** Left: stacked headline + short line + one pill CTA. Right: the globe as the “object” (already there). Give the type more margin; stop crowding chips under the buttons. Mobile: type first, globe still behind or below, not a cramped stack.
4. **Product as object.** Cloth 2-pack / 6-pack cards should sit on wash like Wogan’s bag — white tile, generous padding, photo floating, no yellow clipart.
5. **One button language.** Pill, dark green or lime, not two competing styles in every section.
6. **Air.** Double the section padding. Fewer chips. Kill decorative noise.

## What not to steal

- Mint-only brand (we still need the dark forest photo)
- Illustrated mascot
- Giant empty left column with no photo on mobile
- New serif that fights Fraunces / current heading font — keep current fonts, just scale them

## Pages

Home, shop banner, about, events, contact — same wash-and-air system. Product pages stay white around the gallery.
